About

Hodane Abdi, a Toronto-based analyst working where data, finance and analytics meet.

I bring analytical rigour and clear communication: models tested on data they have never seen, numbers reconciled back to their source, and answers written for the people who have to act on them. I am completing a Master of Management Analytics at Queen's University, Smith School of Business.

I also build the systems behind the numbers: automated pipelines that pull, check and load data without retyping. I use AI tools to work faster, but I own every step, from the logic to the controls to the final answer.

These case studies show how I approach a problem: start with the decision, show the evidence, and be clear about the limits. The code behind every project is public.
